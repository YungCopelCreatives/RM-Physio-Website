Achilles Tendinopathy

Targeted tendon loading protocols for Achilles pain, thickening, and morning stiffness.

Book a session Free 10-min phone call

  3. Achilles Tendinopathy

The Achilles tendon is the thickest and strongest tendon in the body, but repetitive jumping and running loads without adequate rest can cause degenerative tendinopathy. Our structured isometric and heavy slow resistance (HSR) loading protocols stimulate healthy tendon remodeling.

Common causes

  • Sudden increases in running intensity, hill repeats, or sprint work
  • Calf complex weakness (gastrocnemius and soleus)
  • Ankle stiffness and restricted dorsiflexion
  • Sudden transition to zero-drop or minimalist shoes

Symptoms

  • Pain and stiffness in the Achilles tendon on the first morning steps
  • Localized tendon thickening or lump 2-6cm above the heel
  • Pain that warms up with gentle movement but aches significantly after activity
  • Tenderness when squeezing the tendon

How physiotherapy helps

  • Heavy slow resistance (HSR) calf loading protocol
  • Isometric calf holds for immediate tendon pain analgesia
  • Gait and running cadence assessment
  • Temporary heel lift prescription and load management

Prevention tips

  • Gradually condition calves with straight and bent-knee calf raises
  • Warm up thoroughly prior to running or speed drills
  • Avoid rapid changes in shoe heel-to-toe drop
