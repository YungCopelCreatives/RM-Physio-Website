Back Pain

Comprehensive treatment for acute and chronic lumbar, thoracic, and spinal disc pain.

Book a session Free 10-min phone call

Back pain is one of the leading reasons people seek physiotherapy. It ranges from a sharp spasm following lifting to chronic dull lumbar ache from sedentary desk posture. Evidence-based physiotherapy restores spinal mechanics, decompresses irritated joints, activates deep core stabilisers, and prevents chronic recurrence.

Common causes

  • Prolonged static desk sitting, driving, and poor lumbar support
  • Acute muscular spasm or ligament sprain from awkward or heavy lifting
  • Lumbar disc bulges, herniations, or annular tears
  • Facet joint arthritis or spinal stenosis
  • Weakness in transverse abdominis and deep multifidus stabilisers

Symptoms

  • Aching, sharp, or catching pain in the lower or mid back
  • Pain aggravated by prolonged sitting, forward bending, or standing
  • Morning stiffness in the lumbar spine
  • Protective muscle spasms across the flank or buttocks

How physiotherapy helps

  • Spinal joint mobilisations and gentle traction
  • Dry needling for lumbar and quadratus lumborum trigger points
  • Progressive core stabilisation and motor control exercises
  • Postural and ergonomic workstation correction

Prevention tips

  • Maintain active ergonomics and adjust chair height
  • Lift with legs bent and loads held close to your torso
  • Take 60-second micro-breaks every 30-45 minutes

When to seek urgent medical care

  • Loss of bladder or bowel control (Emergency - Cauda Equina)
  • Progressive weakness in legs or saddle area numbness
  • Night pain waking you from sound sleep with fever

Emergency services: 10177 (public) or 082 911 (Netcare 911).
