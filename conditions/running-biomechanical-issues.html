Running Biomechanical Issues

Biomechanical running analysis, cadence retraining, and footwear assessment for runners.

Book a session Free 10-min phone call

  3. Running Biomechanical Issues

Running injuries rarely occur by chance; they are typically rooted in cadence flaws, excessive hip drop (Trendelenburg gait), overstriding, or muscle strength asymmetries. We conduct comprehensive biomechanical assessments to optimize running performance and resolve injuries.

Common causes

  • Overstriding with heavy heel strike increasing braking forces
  • Low running cadence (under 160 steps per minute)
  • Gluteal weakness causing pelvic drop and dynamic knee collapse
  • Calf-Achilles stiffness or inappropriate running shoes

Symptoms

  • Recurrent injuries whenever weekly mileage surpasses a specific threshold
  • Persistent shin splints, plantar fasciitis, or Achilles soreness
  • Hip or lower back stiffness following distance runs
  • Uneven shoe wear patterns and asymmetric running fatigue

How physiotherapy helps

  • Video running gait assessment and cadence optimisation drills
  • Kinetic chain strengthening (soleus, glutes, hamstrings, quads)
  • Footwear selection advice tailored to your foot strike pattern
  • Periodized training volume and return-to-running load management

Prevention tips

  • Gradually increase step rate (cadence) by 5-10% to reduce joint impact
  • Integrate regular single-leg balance and calf capacity workouts
  • Never increase weekly distance by more than 10% at a time
