Conditions we treat

Find your condition to learn the common causes, symptoms and how physiotherapy helps.

Back Pain

Comprehensive treatment for acute and chronic lumbar, thoracic, and spinal disc pain.

Neck Pain

Targeted treatment for cervical stiffness, whiplash, postural strain, and pinched nerves.

Shoulder Pain / Injuries

Specialised care for rotator cuff tears, shoulder impingement, bursitis, and frozen shoulder.

Mobility + Balance Issues

Fall prevention, gait re-education, and neuromuscular stability rehabilitation.

Paediatric + Adult Lung Conditions

Compassionate respiratory care for children and adults with chest infections and sputum retention.

Breathing Impairment Conditions

Rehabilitation for hyperventilation syndrome, dysfunctional breathing, and post-viral shortness of breath.

Sciatica

Specialised treatment for shooting nerve pain radiating from your lower back into your leg.

Runners Knee

Evidence-based rehabilitation for patellofemoral pain syndrome and IT band friction.

Joint Pain

Relief for acute and chronic joint inflammation, cartilage wear, and stiffness.

Achilles Tendinopathy

Targeted tendon loading protocols for Achilles pain, thickening, and morning stiffness.

Arthritis

Pain management, cartilage protection, and functional mobility improvement for arthritic joints.

Oedema (Lymph Drainage)

Manual lymphatic drainage for post-surgical swelling, fluid retention, and oedematous conditions.

Headaches / TMJ (Jaw Issues)

Specialised physiotherapy for jaw clicking, clenching, TMJ pain, and cervicogenic headaches.

Sinus Treatment

Physiotherapy techniques for sinus congestion, facial pressure, and chronic sinusitis.

Cardiac + Lung Intolerance

Cardiopulmonary conditioning for post-cardiac events, exercise fatigue, and shortness of breath.

COPD / Bronchitis / Lung Infections

Chest physiotherapy for airway clearance, chronic bronchitis, COPD, and infection recovery.

Running Biomechanical Issues

Biomechanical running analysis, cadence retraining, and footwear assessment for runners.

Plantar Fasciitis

Heel and arch pain that is worst with your first steps in the morning.
