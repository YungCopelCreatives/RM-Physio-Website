About RM Physiotherapy

A Centurion practice built on clear explanations, focused treatment and genuine follow-up.

Meet Temošo Nkadimeng

HPCSA registered physiotherapist

Temošo is a physiotherapist with experience across musculoskeletal, sports, neurological and respiratory conditions, treating everyone from runners to elderly patients recovering at home. She trained at the University of the Witwatersrand and believes every patient should leave a session understanding their condition and what will get them better.

Credentials

  • BSc Physiotherapy, University of the Witwatersrand (Wits)

What we stand for

We explain everything

Your injury or condition, your prognosis and your management plan, including expected outcomes and realistic timelines.

We address concerns early

We aim to deal with your main concerns in the first session, to ease your anxiety and help you understand your condition.

3 to 4 sessions

We try to resolve impairments within 3 to 4 sessions. If that doesn't happen, we adjust the plan or refer you through the right medical channels.

We work as a team

We collaborate with other qualified healthcare professionals to help you reach your highest functional goals.

Why choose RM Physiotherapy?

High-quality medical care

We work with a full multidisciplinary team so you get a comprehensive, detailed approach to resolving your condition.

Efficient service

We aim to see you within 24 to 48 hours of contacting the practice, with prompt booking and quality care.

Compassionate care

Care goes beyond the consultation room. We follow up after your sessions to track progress and adjust your plan.

Honesty and transparency

We are open and honest with you about your condition, your options and what to expect.

Precise, personalised treatment

A thorough first assessment, then a detailed plan built around your concerns, mobility and lifestyle goals.
