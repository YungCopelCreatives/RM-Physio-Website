About RM Physiotherapy

Dedicated to restoring movement, reducing pain, and enhancing your quality of life through expert physiotherapy care

Our Mission & Vision

Committed to excellence in physiotherapy care and patient outcomes

🎯

Our Mission

To provide exceptional, evidence-based physiotherapy services that empower individuals to achieve optimal movement, function, and well-being. We are committed to delivering personalized care that addresses the unique needs of each patient, whether in our clinic or through convenient home visits.

👁️

Our Vision

To be the leading physiotherapy practice in Pretoria East, recognized for our clinical excellence, innovative treatment approaches, and unwavering commitment to patient-centered care. We envision a community where movement limitations don't define one's potential.

Meet Our Founder

HPCSA registered physiotherapist dedicated to your health and recovery

Temošo Nkadimeng

HPCSA Registered Physiotherapist

Temošo is a passionate and dedicated physiotherapist with extensive experience in treating a wide range of musculoskeletal, neurological, and respiratory conditions. With a commitment to evidence-based practice and continuous professional development, Temošo brings both clinical expertise and genuine care to every patient interaction.

Having worked with diverse patient populations, from elite athletes to elderly individuals, Temošo understands that each person's journey to recovery is unique. This personalized approach, combined with thorough assessment and tailored treatment plans, ensures the best possible outcomes for every patient.

Our Treatment Approach

Evidence-based care tailored to your individual needs and goals

🔍

Comprehensive Assessment

Every treatment begins with a thorough evaluation of your condition, medical history, lifestyle factors, and personal goals to create a complete picture of your needs.

📋

Personalized Treatment Plans

Based on your assessment, we develop individualized treatment plans that incorporate the most effective techniques and exercises for your specific condition and circumstances.

📚

Evidence-Based Practice

Our treatments are grounded in the latest research and best practice guidelines, ensuring you receive the most effective and up-to-date care available in physiotherapy.

🎓

Patient Education

We believe in empowering you with knowledge about your condition, teaching self-management techniques, and providing tools for long-term health and injury prevention.

📊

Progress Monitoring

Regular reassessment and treatment plan adjustments ensure we're on track to meet your goals and maximize your recovery potential throughout the treatment process.

🤝

Holistic Care

We consider all aspects of your health and well-being, working collaboratively with you and other healthcare providers to ensure comprehensive, coordinated care.
