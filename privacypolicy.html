Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the protection of your personal health information is our priority

Last Updated: January 16, 2025

Key Points

  • We collect only necessary information for your healthcare and appointment management
  • Your health information is protected under POPIA and healthcare confidentiality laws
  • We never share your personal information without your explicit consent
  • You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your information
  • Our website uses minimal cookies for functionality and user experience

1. Introduction

RM Physiotherapy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa and other applicable healthcare regulations.

By using our services, website, or providing us with your personal information, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Healthcare Information

  • Personal Details: Name, date of birth, contact information, medical aid details
  • Medical History: Previous injuries, surgeries, current medications, health conditions
  • Treatment Records: Assessment findings, treatment plans, progress notes, exercise programs
  • Emergency Contact: Next of kin or emergency contact information

2.2 Website Information

  • Contact Forms: Information you provide when requesting appointments or information
  • Cookies: Essential cookies for website functionality and user preferences
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data to improve our website (with consent)
  • Communication Records: Email correspondence and appointment scheduling communications

2.3 Third-Party Services

  • Booking System: Information processed through our online appointment booking system
  • Maps: Location data when using our embedded maps for directions
  • Email Services: Contact information for appointment confirmations and newsletters

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Healthcare Purposes

  • Providing physiotherapy assessment, treatment, and care
  • Developing and monitoring treatment plans
  • Coordinating care with other healthcare providers (with your consent)
  • Processing medical aid claims and invoicing
  • Maintaining accurate health records as required by law

3.2 Administrative Purposes

  • Scheduling and managing appointments
  • Sending appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Processing payments and managing accounts
  • Responding to inquiries and providing customer service
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements

3.3 Communication & Marketing

  • Sending health tips and educational content (with consent)
  • Notifying you of service updates or changes
  • Newsletter communications (opt-in basis)
  • Follow-up communications related to your care

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

4.1 Healthcare Providers

We may share your health information with other healthcare professionals involved in your care, including:

  • Your referring doctor or specialist
  • Other physiotherapists providing your care
  • Healthcare facilities where you receive treatment
  • Medical aid schemes for claims processing

Important: Such sharing only occurs with your explicit consent or as required by law.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law, including:

  • Court orders or legal proceedings
  • HPCSA investigations or regulatory requirements
  • Child protection or safety concerns
  • Public health reporting requirements

4.3 Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who may process your information:

  • Booking Platform: Teamup Calendar for appointment scheduling
  • WhatsApp: Booking requests sent from our website forms open in WhatsApp (Meta) and are sent only when you press send
  • Email Services: For appointment confirmations and communications
  • Accounting Software: For invoicing and payment processing
  • IT Support: For data backup and system maintenance

All service providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and POPIA compliance requirements.

5. Data Security & Storage

5.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • Access Controls: Restricted access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular Backups: Secure, encrypted backups of all patient records
  • Physical Security: Locked filing cabinets for paper records and secure IT infrastructure
  • Regular Updates: Software and security systems are regularly updated

5.2 Data Retention

  • Patient Records: Retained for 6 years after last treatment as per HPCSA requirements
  • Financial Records: Retained for 5 years as per tax regulations
  • Communication Records: Email and correspondence retained for 2 years
  • Website Data: Analytics data retained for 2 years, cookies for session duration

5.3 Data Disposal

When retention periods expire, we securely dispose of information by:

  • Secure deletion of digital records
  • Professional shredding of paper documents
  • Certified destruction of storage media
  • Maintaining disposal records as required by law

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

6.1 Access Rights

  • Request access to your personal information we hold
  • Receive a copy of your health records
  • Understand how your information is being used
  • Know who we've shared your information with

6.2 Correction Rights

  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Update your contact details and personal information
  • Provide additional context for your health records

6.3 Deletion Rights

  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Close your patient account (with appropriate notice)

6.4 Objection Rights

  • Object to direct marketing (opt-out at any time)
  • Object to automated decision-making
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances

6.5 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may request identity verification for security purposes.

7. Cookies & Website Technology

7.1 Essential Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Enable booking system functionality
  • Maintain security during your session
  • Provide basic website functionality

7.2 Analytics Cookies

With your consent, we may use analytics cookies to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Improve website design and functionality
  • Identify popular content and services
  • Monitor website performance

You can control cookie settings through our cookie banner and your browser preferences.

7.3 Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps: For location services and directions
  • Teamup Calendar: For appointment booking functionality
  • Font Services: For website typography

8. Children's Privacy

We provide physiotherapy services to children under parental or guardian consent. For patients under 18:

  • Parent/guardian consent is required for all treatment and data processing
  • Parents/guardians have full access rights to their child's information
  • Additional privacy protections apply to sensitive health information
  • Children's information is kept separate from adult patient records

9. International Data Transfers

We primarily store and process your information within South Africa. If international transfers are necessary:

  • Transfers only occur with your explicit consent
  • Adequate protection measures are implemented
  • We ensure POPIA compliance in all transfers
  • Third-party processors meet international privacy standards

10. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect:

  • Changes in legal requirements
  • Updates to our services or technology
  • Improvements to our privacy practices
  • Feedback from patients and regulators

We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • Email notifications to active patients
  • Website notifications and banners
  • Updated policy date at the top of this page
