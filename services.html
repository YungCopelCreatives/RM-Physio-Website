1. Acute and Chronic Pain

We use multiple strategies and treatment modalities to help manage acute and chronic pain

▼

We help with:

  • Acute musculoskeletal spasms and sudden flare-ups
  • Persistent chronic spinal and joint pain
  • Nerve pain and complex regional discomfort
  • Fibromyalgia and generalized myofascial pain
  • Pain science education and self-management strategies

Treatment Modalities:

  • Multimodal pain relief and desensitization
  • Hands-on joint mobilisation and soft tissue release
  • Dry needling for deep neuromuscular trigger points
  • Targeted gradual movement exposure and pacing

2. Orthopaedic Rehabilitation

We assist you before and after orthopaedic surgery

▼

We help with:

  • Pre-operative conditioning (pre-hab) for optimal surgical outcomes
  • Total hip, knee, and shoulder joint replacements (arthroplasty)
  • ACL, meniscus, and rotator cuff tendon repairs
  • Spinal surgery recovery (discectomy, laminectomy, fusion)
  • Post-fracture mobilisation and hardware removal recovery

Rehabilitation Focus:

  • Phase-specific protocol progression coordinated with your surgeon
  • Safe, early swelling reduction and joint range-of-motion restoration
  • Progressive strengthening to reverse post-surgical muscle atrophy
  • Safe return to walking, stair climbing, driving, and daily independence

3. Musculoskeletal Pain

Evidence-based treatment for joint, muscle, and bone pain + conditions

▼

We help with:

  • Spinal pain (lower back, mid-back, and cervical regions)
  • Shoulder impingement, rotator cuff, and frozen shoulder
  • Hip and knee osteoarthritis and biomechanical strain
  • Muscle strains, ligament sprains, and joint stiffness
  • Postural fatigue and repetitive strain injuries (RSI)

Clinical Techniques:

  • Manual therapy, spinal and peripheral joint mobilisations
  • Targeted corrective and therapeutic exercise prescription
  • Postural and ergonomic realignment
  • Long-term injury prevention and durability training

4. Chest Physiotherapy / Lung & Heart Rehabilitation

Respiratory care for adults and children with breathing difficulties

▼

We help with:

  • Paediatric chest infections, viral bronchiolitis, and RSV in infants
  • Adult COPD, chronic bronchitis, and pneumonia recovery
  • Cardiac and pulmonary exercise intolerance
  • Post-operative lung atelectasis and shallow breathing
  • Asthma and reactive airway clearance management

Respiratory Methods:

  • Airway clearance techniques (ACBT) and gentle vibrations
  • Chest percussion, postural drainage, and breathing retraining
  • Listen to paediatric lung sounds with our online sound bites guide
  • Parent guidance on home nebulization and chest care

5. Headache and Jaw Pain (TMJ Syndrome)

Targeted treatment for headaches, jaw pain, clicking, and TMJ syndrome

▼

We help with:

  • Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) dysfunction, jaw clicking, and locking
  • Bruxism (teeth grinding) and stress-related jaw clenching
  • Cervicogenic headaches arising from the upper neck
  • Tension headaches and chronic suboccipital tightness
  • Facial pain and chew-muscle fatigue

TMJ Treatment Approach:

  • Intra-oral and extra-oral pterygoid and masseter muscle release
  • Upper cervical (C1-C3) joint mobilisation
  • Dry needling for facial and neck myofascial trigger points
  • Jaw motor control, tongue posture, and bite re-education

6. Sports Injuries & Running Rehabilitation

We can assist with getting you back to peak performance with specialised sports injury treatment and prevention

▼

We help with:

  • Running biomechanical issues and gait retraining
  • Runner's knee (patellofemoral pain syndrome) and ITB friction
  • Achilles tendinopathy and plantar fasciitis in runners
  • Acute sports ligament sprains (ankle, knee) and muscle tears
  • Gradual return-to-sport physical conditioning

Performance Benefits:

  • Video gait and running mechanics evaluation
  • High-load tendon rehabilitation and plyometric conditioning
  • Sports-specific functional strength and agility drills
  • Reduced risk of re-injury with structured load management

7. Neurological Rehabilitation

We assist specifically with adult stroke rehabilitation; Bell’s palsy and Balance and mobility issues from neurological fall out

▼

We help with:

  • Adult stroke rehabilitation (CVA hemiparesis recovery)
  • Bell's palsy facial nerve rehabilitation and muscle activation
  • Balance and mobility impairments from neurological fall out
  • Parkinson's disease mobility and gait maintenance
  • Peripheral neuropathy and sensory deficits

Neurological Treatment:

  • Neuroplasticity-based movement retraining
  • Targeted facial muscle re-education for Bell's palsy
  • Gait re-education and walking aid selection
  • Dynamic balance and vestibular stabilisation

8. Home Visits

Professional physiotherapy treatment in the comfort of your home (strictly upon special request and booking)

▼

We help with:

  • Servicing Centurion within a 15km radius from the practice
  • Elderly patients requiring fall prevention and mobility care
  • Post-operative patients unable to drive after hip/knee replacement
  • Acute bed-bound or severely incapacitated individuals
  • Paediatric chest physiotherapy in your familiar nursery environment

Home Visit Details:

  • Professional clinical equipment brought directly to your home
  • Travel costs included within service areas (within a 10km radius)
  • Real-world functional assessment of home obstacles and safety
  • Strictly upon special request and booking (terms and conditions apply)

Please note: Home visits are strictly by arrangement with the practice within a 15km radius of Medicross Saxby. Terms and conditions apply.

9. Lymph Drainage + Liposculpture

Post-plastic surgical management / management of oedematous conditions

▼

We help with:

  • Post-plastic surgery healing (liposuction, abdominoplasty, breast surgery)
  • Post-liposculpture fluid retention and fibrosis prevention
  • Chronic oedema and sluggish lymphatic flow
  • Post-traumatic swelling and haematoma reabsorption
  • Post-oncological lymphatic swelling management

Lymphatic Drainage Benefits:

  • Gentle Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) specialised techniques
  • Accelerated recovery and reduction in post-operative swelling/bruising
  • Softening of subcutaneous tissue induration and fibrosis
  • Enhanced tissue regeneration and lymphatic vascular flow
