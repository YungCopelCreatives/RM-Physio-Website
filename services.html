Sports & Running Rehabilitation

Specialized care for athletes and active individuals to optimize performance and prevent injury recurrence

▼

Common Conditions Treated:

  • Running Injuries: IT band syndrome, runner's knee, shin splints, plantar fasciitis
  • Sports-Specific Injuries: Ankle sprains, muscle strains, ligament injuries
  • Overuse Injuries: Tendinopathies, stress fractures, repetitive strain
  • Acute Sports Trauma: Impact injuries, joint dislocations
  • Post-Competition Recovery: Fatigue management, performance optimization

Treatment Approaches:

  • Biomechanical Analysis: Gait assessment, movement pattern correction
  • Manual Therapy: Joint mobilization, soft tissue techniques
  • Exercise Prescription: Progressive strengthening, functional training
  • Performance Enhancement: Plyometric training, sport-specific conditioning
  • Injury Prevention: Pre-season screening, education programs

Expected Outcomes:

  • Faster return to sport and activity
  • Improved biomechanics and movement efficiency
  • Enhanced athletic performance
  • Reduced risk of future injury
  • Better understanding of injury prevention strategies

Musculoskeletal & Orthopaedic

Comprehensive treatment for joint, muscle, and bone conditions using evidence-based approaches

▼

Conditions We Specialize In:

  • Spinal Conditions: Lower back pain, upper back pain, disc problems, sciatica
  • Neck & Shoulder: Neck pain, shoulder impingement, frozen shoulder, whiplash
  • Hip & Knee: Hip arthritis, knee osteoarthritis, patellofemoral pain
  • Post-Surgical Rehabilitation: Joint replacements, ligament repairs, fracture recovery
  • Chronic Pain Conditions: Fibromyalgia, chronic regional pain syndrome

Treatment Techniques:

  • Manual Therapy: Joint mobilization, soft tissue massage, trigger point release
  • Exercise Therapy: Strengthening, stretching, functional movement training
  • Pain Management: Education, pacing strategies, activity modification
  • Postural Correction: Workplace ergonomics, movement re-education
  • Modality Application: Heat, cold, electrotherapy when appropriate

Treatment Goals:

  • Significant pain reduction
  • Improved joint mobility and flexibility
  • Enhanced functional capacity
  • Better posture and movement patterns
  • Prevention of symptom recurrence

Neurological Rehabilitation & TMJ

Specialized care for neurological conditions, balance disorders, headaches, and jaw dysfunction

▼

Neurological Conditions:

  • Balance & Mobility Disorders: Fall prevention, gait abnormalities, dizziness
  • Post-Stroke Recovery: Motor re-learning, functional mobility, coordination
  • Vestibular Disorders: Vertigo, BPPV, balance retraining
  • Movement Disorders: Parkinson's-related movement issues, tremor management
  • Peripheral Neuropathy: Sensation changes, strength deficits

Headache & TMJ Specialization:

  • Headache Types: Tension headaches, cervicogenic headaches, migraines
  • TMJ Disorders: Jaw pain, clicking, grinding, limited mouth opening
  • Facial Pain: Trigeminal neuralgia, facial muscle tension
  • Associated Symptoms: Neck pain, ear symptoms, jaw fatigue

Specialized Treatment Methods:

  • Balance Training: Stability exercises, fall prevention programs
  • Gait Re-education: Walking pattern improvement, mobility aids
  • Manual Therapy: Craniosacral therapy, TMJ mobilization techniques
  • Cognitive-Motor Integration: Dual-task training, attention exercises
  • Vestibular Rehabilitation: Habituation exercises, gaze stabilization
Learn More About Headaches & TMJ

Chest Physiotherapy

Specialized respiratory care for adults and children with breathing difficulties and chest conditions

▼

Adult Respiratory Conditions:

  • COPD Management: Chronic bronchitis, emphysema, breathlessness control
  • Post-Operative Care: Chest surgery recovery, pneumonia prevention
  • Acute Respiratory Issues: Chest infections, pneumonia rehabilitation
  • Breathing Pattern Disorders: Hyperventilation, dysfunctional breathing
  • Asthma Support: Breathing technique optimization, exercise tolerance

Paediatric Chest Care:

  • Childhood Respiratory Conditions: Recurrent chest infections, bronchiolitis
  • Cystic Fibrosis Support: Airway clearance, exercise programs
  • Developmental Issues: Breathing development support, chest wall abnormalities
  • Post-Surgical Care: Recovery from chest or heart surgery

Treatment Techniques:

  • Airway Clearance: Percussion, vibration, postural drainage techniques
  • Breathing Exercises: Diaphragmatic breathing, pursed lip breathing
  • Exercise Programs: Pulmonary rehabilitation, endurance training
  • Education & Support: Disease management, lifestyle modifications
  • Family Training: Teaching caregivers effective techniques

Expected Benefits:

  • Improved breathing efficiency
  • Enhanced exercise tolerance
  • Reduced hospital admissions
  • Better quality of life
  • Effective symptom management

Home Visit Services

Professional physiotherapy treatment delivered in the comfort and convenience of your own home

▼

Service Coverage Areas:

  • Primary Areas: Pretoria East, Pretoria Central, Pretoria North
  • Extended Areas: Midrand, Centurion, Sandton (select areas)
  • Special Arrangements: Other areas considered based on circumstances
  • Travel Time: Efficient scheduling to minimize costs

Ideal Candidates for Home Visits:

  • Mobility Limitations: Difficulty traveling due to pain or disability
  • Post-Surgical Patients: Recent surgery, recovery limitations
  • Elderly Individuals: Preference for familiar environment
  • Busy Professionals: Time constraints, scheduling flexibility needs
  • Parents with Young Children: Childcare considerations

Home Visit Advantages:

  • Convenience: No travel required, familiar environment
  • Personalized Attention: One-on-one care, customized treatment
  • Family Involvement: Education for family members, caregiver training
  • Real-World Assessment: Evaluation in actual living environment
  • Flexible Scheduling: Early morning, evening appointments available

What to Expect:

  • Initial Assessment: Comprehensive evaluation in your home
  • Treatment Planning: Customized program based on your space and needs
  • Equipment Provision: Portable equipment brought as needed
  • Progress Monitoring: Regular reassessment and plan adjustment
  • Education & Training: Self-management techniques, family education

Our Treatment Process

A systematic approach to ensure optimal outcomes for every patient

1

Initial Consultation

Comprehensive assessment including medical history, physical examination, and goal setting to understand your unique situation and needs.

2

Diagnosis & Planning

Analysis of assessment findings to identify the root cause of your condition and develop a personalized, evidence-based treatment plan.

3

Active Treatment

Implementation of your treatment plan using appropriate manual therapy techniques, exercises, and patient education strategies.

4

Progress Monitoring

Regular reassessment and treatment plan adjustments to ensure you're progressing toward your goals effectively and efficiently.

5

Maintenance & Prevention

Transition to self-management with ongoing support, maintenance programs, and strategies to prevent future problems.
