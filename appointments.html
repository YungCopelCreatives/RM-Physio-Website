Free 10-minute telephonic consultation

Not sure whether physiotherapy can help? We offer a complimentary, time-limited phone call to understand your concern and explain how we can help. To book it, send us a WhatsApp or call 064 653 3690 and ask for the free telephonic consultation. We'll arrange a time for the physiotherapist to call you.

Before your visit

Fees

  • Clinic consultation: R850
  • Home visit: R850 plus travel (travel included within 10 km)
  • Package deal: 3 sessions combined for long-term patients. Ask us for details.

Payment and medical aid

  • RM Physiotherapy is a cash-based practice
  • Card (Speedpoint), EFT and cash accepted
  • Our admin submits your claim directly to your medical aid to facilitate reimbursement
  • Bring your physiotherapy approval letter if your plan pre-authorises sessions
  • A detailed statement is provided

First visit preparation

  • Complete the electronic onboarding form (we'll send you the link)
  • Bring previous medical reports or scans
  • Bring referral letters from medical professionals
  • Arrive 10 minutes early if possible

Cancellation policy

  • 24-hour notice required
  • Late cancellation fees may apply
  • No-show fees apply (patient liable)
  • Emergency situations understood
  • Please call if you're running late

Emergencies: For urgent medical emergencies, please contact emergency services immediately: 10177 (public) or 082 911 (Netcare 911).
