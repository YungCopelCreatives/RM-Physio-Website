No. Physiotherapists are primary healthcare practitioners in South Africa; you do not need a doctor's referral to book a consultation. Appointments and Booking

You can book your appointment easily through our online booking button, via our live Teamup Calendar, by calling us at +27 (0) 64 653 3690, or by messaging our team directly on WhatsApp.

Our practice hours are:

  • Monday to Friday: 07h30 – 17h00
  • Saturday: 09h00 – 13h00 (*Appointments essential for weekends)
  • Sunday & Public Holidays: Closed

We kindly request at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment so that another patient in pain may take the slot. Late cancellations or no-shows without 24 hours' prior notice will be subject to a cancellation fee.

To ensure a seamless first visit:

  • Complete your electronic onboarding form: You will receive an electronic intake link before your visit to submit your medical details online.
  • Arrive 10 minutes early: Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time to check in at Medicross Saxby reception.
  • Bring any previous radiology reports (X-rays, MRIs, ultrasound) or specialist referral letters if applicable.

Treatment and What to Expect

Your initial consultation is a comprehensive 45–60 minute session covering:

1. Detailed symptom history & physical movement evaluation

2. Clear differential diagnosis identifying the root cause of your pain

3. Hands-on manual therapy, joint mobilisation, or dry needling if indicated

4. A structured rehabilitation roadmap aiming for resolution within 3–4 sessions, accompanied by a personalised home exercise plan.

Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting athletic or stretchy clothing that allows easy access to the injured area (e.g., shorts for knee, hip, or ankle pain; a tank top or loose shirt for neck and shoulder issues).

We stand for targeted resolution. Rather than scheduling ongoing weekly sessions indefinitely, our average treatment plan aims to resolve acute pain and restore functional independence within 3 to 4 focused sessions. If your condition requires surgery or secondary medical intervention, we coordinate immediate referrals to our medical specialist network.

Fees and Medical Aid

RM Physiotherapy is a CASH-based practice:

  • Payment methods: We accept card (Speedpoint), EFT, and cash immediately following each consultation.
  • Medical aid claims: To save you the administrative hassle, our practice administration submits claims on behalf of patients directly to your medical aid scheme so you receive reimbursement according to your scheme's physiotherapy benefits.
  • Package deal: We offer a discounted package deal combining 3 sessions for long-term rehabilitation patients.
  • Clinic Consultation: R850 (comprehensive 45–60 min assessment & treatment, including dry needling and consumables)
  • Home Visit: R850 (travel costs are fully included within a 10km radius of our Centurion clinic; strictly up to 15km)
  • 3-Session Package Deal: Discounted bundle for long-term and multi-stage rehabilitation patients.

Home Visit Services

We provide mobile home visit physiotherapy across the Centurion area strictly within a 15km radius of our practice at Medicross Saxby Medical Centre in Eldoraigne. Travel costs are fully included within a 10km radius.

Home visits are ideal for post-operative orthopaedic patients (total knee and hip replacements), elderly individuals with mobility challenges or fall risks, neurological patients (stroke recovery), and parents with sick infants needing paediatric chest physiotherapy.
