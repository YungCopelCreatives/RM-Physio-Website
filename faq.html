General Questions

Common questions about our practice and physiotherapy services

Do I need a referral to see a physiotherapist?

▼

No, you don't need a referral to see a physiotherapist in South Africa. You can book directly with us for assessment and treatment. However, some medical aid schemes may require a referral from a doctor for full coverage, so it's worth checking with your medical aid provider first.

If you do need a referral, we can coordinate with your doctor to ensure proper documentation for your medical aid claim.

What should I expect during my first visit?

▼

Your initial consultation is comprehensive and typically lasts 60 minutes. It includes:

  • Medical history review: Discussion of your symptoms, previous injuries, medications, and health conditions
  • Physical examination: Posture assessment, movement tests, strength evaluation, and specific tests for your condition
  • Diagnosis and explanation: Clear explanation of your condition and contributing factors
  • Treatment plan: Customized plan including goals, timeline, and treatment approach
  • Initial treatment: May include some hands-on therapy or exercises
  • Home program: Exercises or self-care techniques to start immediately

How long is each treatment session?

▼

Treatment session lengths vary based on your needs:

  • Initial consultation: 60 minutes (comprehensive assessment and initial treatment)
  • Follow-up treatments: 45 minutes (focused treatment and progress review)
  • Maintenance sessions: 30-45 minutes (depending on your condition)

We ensure adequate time for thorough treatment and to address all your questions and concerns.

What conditions do you treat?

▼

We treat a wide range of conditions across multiple specialties:

  • Musculoskeletal: Back pain, neck pain, shoulder problems, knee pain, hip issues, arthritis
  • Sports injuries: Sprains, strains, overuse injuries, running injuries, return-to-sport rehabilitation
  • Neurological: Stroke recovery, balance problems, Parkinson's support, peripheral neuropathy
  • Respiratory: COPD, chronic bronchitis, post-operative chest care, breathing disorders
  • Headaches & TMJ: Tension headaches, migraines, jaw pain, facial pain
  • Post-surgical: Joint replacements, ligament repairs, fracture recovery

If you're unsure whether we can help with your condition, please call us for a brief discussion.

Are you qualified and registered?

▼

Yes, RM Physiotherapy is operated by Temošo Nkadimeng, a fully qualified and registered physiotherapist:

  • HPCSA Registration: PT 015 3109 (Health Professions Council of South Africa)

  • Professional Indemnity: Fully insured for professional practice
  • Continuing Education: Regular professional development to stay current with best practices

You can verify our registration status on the HPCSA website if needed.

Appointments & Scheduling

Everything you need to know about booking and managing your appointments

How do I book an appointment?

▼

You can book your appointment in several convenient ways:

  • Email: Send your request to info@rmphysiotherapy.com with your preferred dates and times

How far in advance should I book?

▼

Booking timeframes depend on urgency and appointment type:

  • Urgent appointments: Same-day or next-day slots often available
  • Regular appointments: 1-2 weeks in advance recommended
  • Home visits: 2-3 days' notice preferred for scheduling coordination

If you need urgent care, please call us directly, and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

▼

Treatment Questions

Understanding what to expect from your physiotherapy treatment

How many sessions will I need?

▼

The number of sessions varies greatly depending on your condition:

Acute conditions:

  • Simple muscle strains: 3-6 sessions
  • Minor joint sprains: 4-8 sessions
  • Acute back pain: 4-10 sessions

Chronic conditions:

  • Long-term back pain: 8-15+ sessions
  • Arthritis management: Ongoing maintenance
  • Chronic headaches: 6-12 sessions

Post-surgical:

  • Joint replacement: 10-20+ sessions
  • Ligament repair: 15-25+ sessions

We'll discuss your expected treatment timeline during your initial consultation and adjust as needed based on your progress.

Will treatment be painful?

▼

Our approach prioritizes your comfort while ensuring effective treatment:

What to expect:

  • Some discomfort during assessment is normal
  • Manual therapy may cause mild, temporary soreness
  • Exercises should be challenging but not painful
  • Post-treatment soreness may occur (usually resolves in 24-48 hours)

Our philosophy:

  • No treatment should cause severe pain
  • We adjust techniques based on your tolerance
  • Communication is key - always tell us if something hurts
  • We explain what we're doing and why

If you're particularly sensitive or anxious about pain, please let us know so we can adjust our approach accordingly.

What should I wear to my appointment?

▼

Appropriate clothing helps us assess and treat you effectively:

General guidelines:

  • Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing
  • Clothing that allows easy movement
  • Layers that can be easily removed
  • Supportive, flat shoes (avoid high heels)

For specific areas:

  • Back/spine: Top that can be lifted or removed, loose pants
  • Shoulder/arm: Tank top or t-shirt that can be removed
  • Hip/leg: Shorts or loose pants, sports bra for women
  • Neck: Low neckline or removable top

We provide:

  • Gowns or draping for modesty
  • Private changing areas
  • Professional, respectful environment

Will I need to do exercises at home?

▼

Home exercises are a crucial part of your recovery:

Why home exercises matter:

  • Accelerate your recovery between sessions
  • Maintain improvements made during treatment
  • Prevent re-injury once treatment is complete
  • Empower you with self-management skills

What we provide:

  • Clear, written instructions with pictures
  • Demonstration during your session
  • Progressive programs that advance with your improvement
  • Modifications for your home environment

Time commitment:

  • Usually 10-20 minutes, 1-3 times daily
  • Quality over quantity - proper form is essential
  • We'll adjust the program if it's too difficult or time-consuming

Can I continue my regular activities during treatment?

▼

Activity modification is part of your treatment plan:

General principles:

  • Stay as active as possible within pain limits
  • Avoid activities that significantly worsen your condition
  • Modify rather than completely stop activities when possible
  • Gradually return to normal activities as you improve

Work activities:

  • Most people can continue working with modifications
  • We can provide workplace assessment and recommendations
  • Medical certificates provided if rest is needed
  • Return-to-work programs for complex cases

Sports and exercise:

  • Sport-specific return programs
  • Alternative exercises during recovery
  • Injury prevention strategies
  • Performance optimization once recovered

Conditions & Specialties

Questions about specific conditions and treatment approaches

Do you treat sports injuries?

▼

Yes, sports and running rehabilitation is one of our specialties:

Common sports injuries we treat:

  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Knee injuries (ligament, meniscus, patellofemoral)
  • Shoulder impingement and rotator cuff injuries
  • Running injuries (IT band, plantar fasciitis, shin splints)
  • Muscle strains and tears
  • Tennis elbow and golfer's elbow

Our approach includes:

  • Comprehensive biomechanical assessment
  • Sport-specific rehabilitation programs
  • Return-to-sport protocols
  • Injury prevention strategies
  • Performance optimization

Can you help with chronic pain conditions?

▼

Yes, we have extensive experience managing chronic pain conditions:

Chronic conditions we treat:

  • Chronic lower back pain
  • Neck pain and headaches
  • Arthritis and joint stiffness
  • Fibromyalgia
  • Chronic regional pain syndrome
  • Persistent post-surgical pain

Our chronic pain approach:

  • Pain education and understanding
  • Gradual activity progression
  • Movement optimization
  • Stress and sleep management
  • Long-term self-management strategies
  • Coordination with other healthcare providers

Do you provide chest physiotherapy?

▼

Yes, chest physiotherapy is available for both adults and children:

Adult respiratory conditions:

  • COPD and chronic bronchitis
  • Post-operative chest care
  • Pneumonia recovery
  • Breathing pattern disorders
  • Asthma management support

Paediatric chest care:

  • Recurrent chest infections
  • Cystic fibrosis support
  • Developmental breathing issues
  • Post-surgical respiratory care

Treatment techniques:

  • Airway clearance techniques
  • Breathing exercises and retraining
  • Chest percussion and vibration
  • Postural drainage
  • Exercise programs for lung health

Do you treat neurological conditions?

▼

Conditions we treat:

  • Stroke recovery and rehabilitation
  • Balance and mobility disorders
  • Parkinson's disease support
  • Peripheral neuropathy
  • Vestibular disorders and vertigo
  • Post-concussion management

Treatment focus areas:

  • Balance and coordination training
  • Gait re-education
  • Strength and endurance building
  • Fall prevention programs
  • Functional mobility improvement
  • Cognitive-motor integration

We work closely with neurologists and other specialists to provide comprehensive care.
