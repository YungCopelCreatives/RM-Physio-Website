How we can help

Ten services, one goal: getting you moving without pain.

Acute and Chronic Pain

We use multiple strategies and treatment modalities to help manage acute and chronic pain

▼

We help with:

  • Acute musculoskeletal spasms and sudden flare-ups
  • Persistent chronic spinal and joint pain
  • Nerve pain and complex regional discomfort
  • Fibromyalgia and generalized myofascial pain
More on this service →

Musculoskeletal Pain

Evidence-based treatment for joint, muscle, and bone pain + conditions

▼

We help with:

  • Spinal pain (lower back, mid-back, and cervical regions)
  • Shoulder impingement, rotator cuff, and frozen shoulder
  • Hip and knee osteoarthritis and biomechanical strain
  • Muscle strains, ligament sprains, and joint stiffness
More on this service →

Chest Physiotherapy / Lung & Heart Rehabilitation

Respiratory care for adults and children with breathing difficulties

▼

We help with:

  • Paediatric chest infections, viral bronchiolitis, and RSV in infants
  • Adult COPD, chronic bronchitis, and pneumonia recovery
  • Cardiac and pulmonary exercise intolerance
  • Post-operative lung atelectasis and shallow breathing
More on this service →

Headache and Jaw Pain (TMJ Syndrome)

Targeted treatment for headaches, jaw pain, clicking, and TMJ syndrome

▼

We help with:

  • Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) dysfunction, jaw clicking, and locking
  • Bruxism (teeth grinding) and stress-related jaw clenching
  • Cervicogenic headaches arising from the upper neck
  • Tension headaches and chronic suboccipital tightness
More on this service →

Sports Injuries & Running Rehabilitation

We can assist with getting you back to peak performance with specialised sports injury treatment and prevention

▼

We help with:

  • Running biomechanical issues and gait retraining
  • Runner's knee (patellofemoral pain syndrome) and ITB friction
  • Achilles tendinopathy and plantar fasciitis in runners
  • Acute sports ligament sprains (ankle, knee) and muscle tears
More on this service →

Home Visits

Professional physiotherapy treatment in the comfort of your home (strictly upon special request and booking)

▼

We help with:

  • Servicing Centurion within a 15km radius from the practice
  • Elderly patients requiring fall prevention and mobility care
  • Post-operative patients unable to drive after hip/knee replacement
  • Acute bed-bound or severely incapacitated individuals
More on this service →

Why choose RM Physiotherapy?

High-quality medical care

We work with a full multidisciplinary team so you get a comprehensive, detailed approach to resolving your condition.

Efficient service

We aim to see you within 24 to 48 hours of contacting the practice, with prompt booking and quality care.

Compassionate care

Care goes beyond the consultation room. We follow up after your sessions to track progress and adjust your plan.

Honesty and transparency

We are open and honest with you about your condition, your options and what to expect.

Precise, personalised treatment

A thorough first assessment, then a detailed plan built around your concerns, mobility and lifestyle goals.
