Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about appointments and services

How far in advance should I book my appointment?

▼

We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance for regular appointments. For urgent concerns, we often have same-day or next-day availability. Home visits may require a few days' notice for scheduling coordination.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

▼

No referral is needed for physiotherapy treatment in South Africa. However, some medical aid schemes may require a referral for full coverage, so check with your provider. We can also coordinate with your doctor if needed.

What should I expect during my first visit?

▼

Your initial consultation includes a detailed assessment, medical history review, physical examination, and development of your treatment plan. The first visit typically lasts 60 minutes and may include some initial treatment techniques.

How much do treatments cost?

▼

Treatment costs vary depending on the service type and location. We offer transparent pricing and can provide quotes over the phone. Most medical aid schemes cover physiotherapy, and we offer direct billing for approved schemes.

Are home visits more expensive than clinic visits?

▼

Home visits include a travel fee to cover time and transportation costs, but this is often offset by the convenience and personalized care. Many patients find the value excellent, especially those with mobility limitations or busy schedules.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

▼

Please provide 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. You can easily reschedule through our online booking system, or call us directly. We understand that emergencies happen and will work with you when possible.
